Method 1: Harmonic Mean Formula (Recommended)
⚡ Fastest & Most Direct

When distance for both legs is equal, average speed is: 2ab / (a + b). This accounts for differing travel times automatically.

2
Method 2: Total Distance ÷ Total Time (First Principles)
📚 Best for Exam Proofs

Calculate time for leg 1: t₁ = d/60. Calculate time for leg 2: t₂ = d/40. Total time t = t₁ + t₂ and divide total distance 2d by t.

📐 Step-by-Step Breakdown:
1. Time taken for outbound trip (60 km/h): 120 km ÷ 60 km/h = 2 hours.
2. Time taken for return trip (40 km/h): 120 km ÷ 40 km/h = 3 hours.
3. Total distance: 120 + 120 = 240 km.
4. Total time: 2 + 3 = 5 hours.
5. Average speed: 240 km ÷ 5 hours = 48 km/h.
💡 Key Takeaway:

The simple average of 60 and 40 is 50, but because the train spends more time at 40 km/h (3 hours vs 2 hours), the slower speed carries more weight.
